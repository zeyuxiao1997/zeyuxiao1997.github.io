Brief Bio
I am Zeyu Xiao, currently a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, National University of Singapore (NUS), working with Prof. Xinchao Wang. I received the Ph.D. degree from the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), supervised by Prof. Zhiwei Xiong, and the B.E. degree from Hefei University of Technology (HFUT). My research interests include computer vision and machine learning.
