Brief Bio
I am Zeyu Xiao, currently a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, National University of Singapore (NUS). I received my Ph.D. degree from the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), and my B.E. degree from Hefei University of Technology (HFUT). My research interests include low-level vision, computational imaging, and multimodal intelligence.
Research Interests
My research focuses on three interconnected directions:
  • Low-Level Vision: Image and video restoration, enhancement, super-resolution, and video processing.
  • Computational Imaging: Event-based vision, light-field imaging, neuromorphic sensing, and image reconstruction.
  • Multimodal Intelligence: Multimodal learning, vision-language models, and robust visual understanding and reasoning.
